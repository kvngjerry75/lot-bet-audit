## C. Machine numbers (489 draws with machine numbers, 2025-12-09 to 2026-10-26)

- Pink machine numbers: 1302 (2.66 per draw). If they were simply exits 7-12 of the same machine: 1507 (z = -8.1, p = 3.7e-16). If picked evenly from the leftover balls: 1433.
- All 12 balls together are 47.9% pink (a fair machine: 49.1%). So the machine takes out about a fair 12; the pink ones simply tend to come out FIRST (winning numbers), the yellow ones later (machine numbers).
- Pink share by exit position 1-12: 0.48 0.54 0.53 0.54 0.50 0.51 0.48 0.46 0.48 0.44 0.39 0.41
- Within each colour, a ball's winning rate vs its machine-number rate: r = -0.422 (null of a fair 12 with pink first and NO per-ball effect: mean -0.018, p = 0.005). Negative = balls that win more come out LESS as machine numbers: each ball has its own 'comes out early' tendency.
- Money: tested walk-forward (an 'early ball' model using the machine numbers), it did NOT beat sharp (see the audit report), and machine-number Perm-2 at 20x returns about 22p per GH₵1.
